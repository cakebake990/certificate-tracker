# MVP Scope

## Planned capabilities

- **Certificate inventory:** maintain one governed record per certificate and relate its operational context.
- **Certificate search and detail:** find a certificate and understand its relevant attributes and impact.
- **Expiration dashboard/work queue:** identify certificates approaching expiration and support team-owned action.
- **Add Certificate wizard:** capture a new certificate quickly with a review step.
- **Update/Replace Certificate wizard:** guide renewal or replacement work without losing entered values on back navigation.
- **Adaptive update guidance:** show relevant operational prompts only when applicable.
- **Support case reference:** associate, but do not replace or create, the external support case.
- **Follow-up actions:** capture unfinished operational work.
- **History/audit:** review material certificate changes and update activity.

Detailed workflows, fields, roles, thresholds, acceptance criteria, retention, and audit semantics remain subject to future refinement.

## Future or separate scope

Certificate deployment, private-key/secrets management, automatic discovery, endpoint validation, Java trust-store and SFTP distribution, ServiceNow integration or case creation, advanced notifications/escalations, and ML/AI recommendations are outside the MVP. See [Known Gaps and Future Scope](../01-governance/KNOWN_GAPS_AND_FUTURE_SCOPE.md).
